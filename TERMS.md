## Program Description

bunni is an AI-powered calendar assistant you use by text message. Text our number in plain English to create and manage Google Calendar events.

Your messages are processed by an AI model (Claude, by Anthropic) in order to understand what you asked for. See our Privacy Policy for what is shared and with whom.

## How to Use

Text **+18669647689** to get started. You will receive a link to connect your Google Calendar. The link works once and expires after 15 minutes; text us again for a fresh one.

Once connected, text us anything like "meeting with Jake Friday at 3pm" and we will add it to your calendar.

## Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your usage.

You will normally receive one reply per message sent. If you send an unusual volume of messages in a short period, we may stop replying until the next hour.

The service also runs under a monthly operating budget. If that budget is reached, we will tell you once and then stop replying until the start of the next month. STOP and HELP keep working either way.

**We also text you automatically about upcoming events** — about an hour before a timed event starts, and again as it begins. These apply to timed events on the calendar you connect, and are not configured per event. All-day events do not trigger them.

Opting out is permanent and cannot be undone. Texting us again afterwards starts a new account, and you will need to reconnect your Google Calendar.

## Google Calendar Access

By connecting your Google Calendar, you authorize bunni to read and write calendar events on your behalf.

We ask before anything irreversible: deleting an event always asks you to confirm first and names the event, and adding or moving something onto an occupied slot asks first and names what it collides with.

If you ask us to invite someone, we read the email address back to you and wait for you to confirm before anything is sent. Google then emails that person an invitation on your behalf. An invitation cannot be recalled once it has gone out, so check the address. You are responsible for who you invite.

You can revoke this access at any time through your Google Account settings at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), or by replying STOP.
